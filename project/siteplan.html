Website Plan

CoinBest — Learn, Collect & Trade Coins

01 Site Name

CoinBest

The name combines coin with best, signaling two things at once: this is a place to find the best coins available, and the best place to learn about the hobby. It's short, easy to say out loud, and works equally well for a learning resource and a storefront — which matches the site's dual role.

Optional domain availability: coinbest.com

02 Site Purpose

CoinBest has a dual purpose: it is a place to learn about and share the coin collecting hobby, and a functional e-commerce hub to buy, sell, and trade historical coins. Alongside a browsable product catalog with condition grading and pricing, and a dedicated intake form for selling coins, the site offers educational content — grading guides, coin history, and collecting tips — so newcomers can understand the hobby before they buy or sell anything.

03 Scenarios

Scenarios represent questions a target visitor — a collector, a newcomer to the hobby, or someone who inherited coins — might bring to the site.

  • "I'm new to coin collecting. Where do I even start, and what should I learn first?"
  • "I have a box of old coins from my grandfather. How do I find out what they're worth and sell them?"
  • "I collect U.S. Morgan dollars. What condition is this one in, and how much does it cost?"
  • "What do grading codes like MS-65 or VF-30 actually mean?"

04 Color Schema

The palette is drawn from the coin shop's own materials — oxidized copper and bronze, brass detailing, and aged ledger paper — instead of a generic storefront look.

05 Typography

Three typefaces cover three distinct jobs: a characterful display serif for headings, a readable serif for body copy, and a monospace face for prices, grades, and labels — mimicking a certification tag.
